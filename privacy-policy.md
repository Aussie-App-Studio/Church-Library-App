# Privacy Policy for Church Library

**Last Updated:** October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the **Church Library** mobile application ("the App"), developed and maintained by **Aussie App Studio**. The App is designed to assist churches and local communities in managing book lending, cataloging, and local inventory.

### 1. Information Collection and Storage

- **Local Storage Only:** All library catalog data, book records, borrow histories, and patron contact details (such as names or phone numbers entered for book borrowing) are stored locally on your device in a secure on-device database.
- **No External Servers:** We do not operate cloud servers, external user databases, or third-party analytical tracking services. Your data remains strictly within your organization's control.

### 2. Device Permissions and Usage

The App requests certain permissions strictly to provide local functionality:

- **Camera / Barcode Scanner:** Used solely to scan book barcodes (ISBN barcodes) for quick cataloging, checkout, and returns. No images or video feeds are captured, stored, or transmitted over the internet.
- **Internet Access (Book Metadata & Covers):** Used solely to query public book catalog APIs (**Google Books API** and **Open Library API**) using book ISBNs, titles, or authors to automatically retrieve book metadata and cover images. No patron records or personal data are ever transmitted in these requests.
- **Local Network / Wi-Fi / Bluetooth / Nearby Devices:** Used exclusively for direct peer-to-peer synchronization between church library tablets/devices on the same local network. No data leaves the local network.
- **Location (Fine/Coarse Location):** Required by older Android operating system versions to discover and connect to nearby Wi-Fi and Bluetooth devices for local synchronization. The App does **not** track, record, or share your physical location.
- **Notifications:** Used solely for local system notifications, such as displaying the status of local tablet synchronization.

### 3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

- We do **not** sell, rent, trade, or share any personal information or library records with third parties.
- Aside from anonymous book ISBN/title lookups to Google Books and Open Library for cover art and metadata, the App makes no external network requests.
- We do **not** use third-party analytics (like Google Analytics or Firebase) or advertising networks.

### 4. Data Security & Backups

Data backups (JSON or CSV export files) generated by the App are created locally on the device at the administrator's request and can be saved or transferred only by the authorized user.

### 5. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 online. Any patron records created are stored strictly on the local device and managed locally by the church library administrator.

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with a revised "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.

### 7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact **Aussie App Studio** by opening an issue at our GitHub repository: [https://github.com/Aussie-App-Studio/Church-Library-App](https://github.com/Aussie-App-Studio/Church-Library-App).
